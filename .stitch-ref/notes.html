System Design: Uber Dispatcher

The objective of the dispatcher service is to match a rider's request with the nearest available driver. The system must handle high throughput and remain responsive even during local flash traffic (concerts, sporting events).

"Latency is king here. A match that takes 30 seconds is a lost customer." — Interviewer Note

Core Components

  • Geo-Index: Using Google S2 library or Quadtrees to segment the map into manageable cells.
  • Websockets: To maintain persistent connections with both driver and rider apps for real-time updates.
  • Redis: For ephemeral storage of driver locations, updated every 4 seconds.

We discussed handling "hot spots" where thousands of people request a ride at once. The solution proposed was to distribute the load across multiple cell-shards and use a distributed locking mechanism for driver allocation to prevent double-booking.
